4. Video box, size and position

Left/right and up/down only matter when the video is bigger than its box. Smaller videos are centered on white. With "match the box height" on, a normal video fits its box exactly at 100%.

Removes the logo's own background so it blends into the white. Raise the strength if a faint box or halo is left; lower it if parts of the logo start to disappear. The page background is always white.
